Why there are two harness configurations

The original 48 KB local request guard rejected complete Jev inputs. A development-only probe demonstrated that the service accepted a larger request. A separately frozen follow-up raised the local guard to 96 KB and ran all 36 harness attempts afresh. The original failures remain visible; none were replaced with favorable retries. Raising the cap can also change how many subjects are scored in one request.

The amendment was chosen before inspecting reference accuracy. It uses the same images and all 36 original controls, so it is a configuration follow-up, not a new independent validation cohort. Read the amendment.

What the numbers mean and what they cannot establish
  • Conditional MAPE: mean absolute percentage count error among returned counts; lower is better. It excludes missing answers, so read it with coverage.
  • Mask F1: instance matching at IoU ≥ 0.5, averaged over returned masks. A correct total can still hide missed and extra objects.
  • Review status: provisional answers remain provisional. A later API/planning failure may preserve an earlier reviewed answer and its matching mask.
  • Runtime: means use recorded controller durations. Vanilla Astra: 36/36 runtimes recorded; Full harness · 48 KB: 36/36 runtimes recorded; Full harness · 96 KB: 36/36 runtimes recorded.
  • The two harness jobs ran concurrently with four and three workers, respectively, using the same per-trial CPU/memory caps. Timing is observational under this load.
  • The full harness has original 16-bit pixels, research, code and specialist inference. This comparison cannot isolate Jev's contribution, establish broad multimodal superiority or validate medical use.
  • Data: BBBC039. This counting task is derived from reference segmentation masks; it is not the dataset's original published score. Public pretrained exposure is unknown.
