Vision harness · routing v1 · 22 September 2026

Can a router choose the right specialist?

Six additional official BBBC039 test images. Two pretrained model families, four registered procedures, one frozen Astra preference and a Jev score for every eligible procedure.

This is a new review policy. The final harness preserves grounded estimates with a separate supported or caution label. Historical full-harness runs required review approval to return a count. Their completion rates are not directly comparable.

What this run established

The specialist capability is useful, but this routing policy did not beat vanilla Astra. Cellpose-SAM was the best fixed procedure at 4.23% mean count error; vanilla scored 5.44% and the final routed harness 5.71%. All six routed results carry a caution flag. Jev selected a best-performing procedure on two of six images. These are exploratory results on this cohort, not universal model rankings.

Scope and reproduction

These are real benchmark images, with reference counts decoded from published annotations only after inference. The previous cohort was excluded. Public pretrained-data exposure is unknown; this is a small exploratory test, not independent validation of a universal router. Derived count MAPE is not the published BBBC039 segmentation metric.

The specialists ran in separate native environments on the Mac; Astra and Jev used hosted APIs. Harbor ATIF traces were exported and validated. This was not a Harbor container evaluation. Jev received structured evidence, not image pixels.
